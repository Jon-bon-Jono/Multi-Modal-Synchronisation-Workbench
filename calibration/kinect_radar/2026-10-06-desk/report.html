Apply to Kinect skeleton points

For native Kinect SDK XYZ in millimetres, divide by 1000. Then either reorder to (x,z,−y) and use kinect_to_radar_4x4, or use native_kinect_to_radar_4x4 directly without reordering. The cap offset is specific to calibration labels and must not be applied to skeleton joints.

Reproduce

From the project root using the etl environment; choose a new output directory:

python tools/adc_viewer/calibrate_desk.py sessions calibration_results/new-run --date 2026-10-06 --timezone Australia/Sydney --allow-incomplete-kinect-session desk_left_2
